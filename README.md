### About the Experiment 

Riveting is a popular method of fastening and joining because of its simplicity, dependability and low cost. Dissimilar metals and assemblies having a number of parts with non-uniform thickness can readily be fastened. These joints are made in location where they either do not increase weight of the work at all (over the weight of an integral work) or increase it negligibly.
